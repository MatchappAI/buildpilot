# Phone, glasses & alerts

On the **Connect** tab:

- **Set up notifications**: alerts like *"Claude needs your approval"* to your phone (free **ntfy** app), Slack or Discord. Glasses paired to your phone can announce them.
- **Pocket**: turn on the Integration API and open the Pocket link on your phone. It shows every session and can **read updates aloud** through Bluetooth glasses or earbuds.
