# Your mission control

BuildPilot lives in the **Activity Bar** (the compass icon). It answers three questions at a glance:

1. **What's happening?** Every Claude Code session, live.
2. **Is anything wrong?** Failing tests, loops, leaked secrets, two agents in the same file.
3. **What should I do next?** One recommended step, with a ready-made prompt.

Everything stays on your computer.
