# Write better prompts

Open **Prompts** (or press **⌘⌥P**). Pick a goal (*Fix a bug*, *Connect a service*, *Get ready to ship*…) and describe what you want in plain English.

BuildPilot adds your project's context, guardrails, clear *done means* criteria, and the exact commands Claude must run to prove it works.

Then **Copy** it, or **Start in Claude Code** to open a terminal with it.
